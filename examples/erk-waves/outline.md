# Outline — confirmed scientific narrative

## 1. Question
Known: ERK waves organize collective epithelial migration.
Unknown: what sustains and propagates them.
Question: is mechanical deformation alone a sufficient feedback?

## 2. Puzzle / Gap
Existing view: purely chemical reaction-diffusion models reproduce a wave.
But: they put ERK activity behind strain — the wrong sign vs. measurement.

## 3. Observation
Claim: ERK activation precedes cell extension by a fixed ~20 min lag.
Evidence: cross-correlation of ERK and strain channels (Fig 1).

## 4. Mechanism
Two variables (ERK E, strain s) + one mechanical feedback term f(s).
Hopf bifurcation -> oscillation; neighbor coupling -> traveling wave.

## 5. Prediction
H1 fixed phase lag; H2 speed grows with gain; H3 feedback block -> arrest.

## 6. Test
Claim: model matches phase lag and wave speed with zero fitted parameters.
Evidence: comparison table (Experiment vs Model).

## 8. Conclusion
Take-home: a single mechanical feedback sustains ERK waves and predicts the phase lead.
